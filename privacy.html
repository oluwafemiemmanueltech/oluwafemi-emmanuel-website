Privacy notice

How this website handles information.

This draft describes the current static portfolio implementation. Review it for your actual business and applicable legal requirements before publishing.

Last reviewed: 10 November 2026

Information submitted through forms

The website has no form-processing server or database. When you complete a contact, consultation, or website-preview form, the page prepares an email draft addressed to oluwafemiemmanueltech@gmail.com. The draft is not sent until you choose to send it in your email application. If you send it, the information is handled by your email provider and the recipient's email service.

Information stored by this website

This static website does not intentionally store form entries, set analytics cookies, or run advertising trackers. The local development server is intended for use on the same computer. A public hosting provider may record technical access logs under its own policies.

External services

Links to GitHub, LinkedIn, and email open services outside this website. Those providers handle information according to their own privacy notices. No external calendar, CRM, payment processor, or analytics service is connected by this implementation.

Your choices

You may contact me directly at oluwafemiemmanueltech@gmail.com with privacy questions. Do not put sensitive personal or financial information in a project enquiry.

Updates

This notice must be updated if a form backend, calendar, analytics, CRM, or payment integration is added. Until then, it describes only this static site and should be treated as a draft for review.
